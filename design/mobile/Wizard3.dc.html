Step 3 of 5 · Backups
Keep a copy of your books
Your books live only on this phone. If it's lost or replaced, so are they, unless there's a copy somewhere.
Keep copies in iCloud Drive
A timestamped copy each day you use the app
Copies go to your own iCloud Drive, in a Duesbook folder you can see in the Files app. That is the only place anything ever leaves this phone, and you can turn it off any time in Settings.
This phone's own backup covers Duesbook too, if iCloud Backup is on. The copies above are the extra insurance you can hand to the next treasurer.
